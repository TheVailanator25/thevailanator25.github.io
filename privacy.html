Privacy

EVE Personal Assistant is a Windows application developed and operated by Vailan Kita for personal use. This policy describes the current application and this informational website.

Information EVE accesses

When you connect an account, EVE verifies the authenticated mailbox and uses the permissions you grant to read recent email conversations and upcoming calendar events. This includes sender and recipient details, subjects, dates, message text and calendar event details. You can also provide tasks, notes, preferences, account labels and voice recordings.

How information is used

EVE uses connected information to suggest email follow-up tasks, display calendar events, organise priorities and generate requested summaries and plans. Current Google and Microsoft permissions are read-only. EVE does not send email or create calendar invitations through these connections.

AI processing and service providers

Selected email conversation text and imported notes are sent to the owner's signed-in OpenAI Codex service for classification and summarisation. Voice recordings started by the owner are sent to Groq for transcription. These providers process information under their applicable terms and account settings. See OpenAI's privacy policy and Groq's privacy policy.

EVE does not sell personal information, use it for advertising or operate its own model-training service. Google account information is used for the connected assistant features described here.

Storage

EVE stores task records, notes, summaries, calendar details, preferences and activity history on the owner's computer. OAuth grants and API credentials are encrypted using Windows through Electron safeStorage. EVE does not encrypt the task and notes database or ordinary exported backups.

The mailbox integration processes email bodies in memory and retains follow-up decisions and source links locally. Information extracted into tasks or summaries remains in those records. Codex may maintain its own local or service records according to its configuration.

Your controls and retention

You can disable background checks or disconnect an account in EVE Settings. Disconnecting removes that account's stored EVE grant and cached calendar entries. Previously saved follow-up tasks remain until you manage them separately. You can also revoke EVE's access in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Local information remains until you remove it. App updates do not automatically delete it, and you manage exported backups separately. Contact the developer below for help with removing EVE's local information or questions about this policy.

This website

This is a static informational website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no account login, forms, advertising or analytics added by EVE. Visiting it does not connect an email account or enable EVE. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site under the GitHub privacy statement.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated as EVE's features or data practices change. A future public product or plugin may require additional disclosures and permissions.
