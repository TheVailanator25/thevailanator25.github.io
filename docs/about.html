EVE PERSONAL ASSISTANT

A little more space
for your day.

EVE is a personal Windows assistant being developed by Vailan Kita. She brings tasks, email follow-ups, calendar events and meeting actions into one place.

What EVE does

EVE helps organise priorities, find email conversations that may need a reply, view upcoming calendar events, extract actions from imported meeting notes and put together a plan for the day. You can interact through text or voice and review the actions she suggests.

Accounts you choose

Connecting a Google or Microsoft account allows EVE to read email conversations and calendar events for those features. The current permissions are read-only. EVE cannot send email, delete messages or change events through these connections.

Made for your desktop

EVE runs on the owner's Windows computer, with local task and note storage. Connected services provide email, calendar access, AI processing and voice transcription. Background checks run while EVE and the computer are running.

Still taking shape

EVE is currently in private development. Public downloads, account registration and an installable plugin are not available yet. This website introduces the project and explains how the current application handles information.
