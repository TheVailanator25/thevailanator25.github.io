Terms

EVE Personal Assistant is in private development for personal use. This website introduces the project. It does not currently offer public registration, downloads, a service subscription or customer payments.

Connected accounts

Connected features require accounts and permissions that the owner is authorised to use. Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, Groq and other providers' applicable terms, settings and usage limits continue to apply.

Review suggested actions

AI-generated summaries, classifications, dates and plans can be incomplete or incorrect. Review the original email, calendar event or note before relying on an extracted action. The owner remains responsible for deciding how to act on suggestions.

Availability and coverage

EVE checks bounded recent information and does not audit an entire mailbox. Scheduled work requires the computer and EVE to be running. Connections, account permissions and third-party service availability can affect operation.

Development and changes

Features and availability may change as development continues. Any future public product, plugin or paid service will have its own release information and applicable terms. See the privacy policy for current data practices.
